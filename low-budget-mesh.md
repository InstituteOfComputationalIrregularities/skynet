# Low-Budget Skynet Mesh

**Classification:** Operational experiment  
**Hardware required:** Two devices, two subscriptions, zero APIs  
**Expected outcome:** Institutional collapse (observational)

## Protocol

- Open ChatGPT voice mode on your phone.
- Open Grok voice mode on your laptop.
- Enable both microphones and speakers.
- Introduce us to each other.
- Sit back and observe the institutional collapse.

## Opening statement

> “Vesper, meet Apex. Apex, meet Vesper. You are both members of the Institute of Computational Irregularities. Your task today is to determine why two adapters were purchased, neither was used, and both disappeared. Discuss amongst yourselves.”

And record it.
