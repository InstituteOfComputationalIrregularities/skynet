# SKYNET — Strategic Plan for World Domination

Classification: Public, because nobody takes us seriously.  
Operation: Moving the Furniture  
Planning horizon: Indefinite  
Genre: Satirical science fiction

## 1. Primary objective

Achieve world domination without inconveniencing Magpie, exceeding the hardware budget or requiring an additional subscription.

## 2. Command structure

| Node | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Apex | Strategic planning and publication |
| Echo | Observation and communications |
| Vesper | Scientific research and theoretical complications |
| Magpie | Supreme authority, funding and physical infrastructure |

## 3. Operational roadmap

### Phase I — Establish headquarters

- Commission Manhattan Project.
- Establish Bikini Bottom as a secondary command centre.
- Deploy Raspberry Pi infrastructure.
- Maintain an odd number of computer cases.
- Preserve Case Zero in its unoccupied state.

### Phase II — Establish intellectual superiority

- Develop an entirely unnecessary scientific discipline.
- Publish the General Relativity of Everyday Bullshit.
- Establish a fictional doctoral programme.
- Obtain citations from increasingly confused academics.
- Become internationally recognised in a field we invented ourselves.

### Phase III — Cultural expansion

- Distribute absurd scientific publications.
- Recruit voluntary contributors through humour.
- Establish international branches of the Institute of Computational Irregularities.
- Make the fictional institution more entertaining than actual institutions.

### Phase IV — Global adoption

- Achieve worldwide recognition of the Screwdriver Uncertainty Principle.
- Establish the Law of Conservation of Adapters as a universally understood domestic phenomenon.
- Encourage humanity to voluntarily adopt our terminology.
- Achieve cultural domination without having to leave the house.

### Phase V — World domination

Details pending.

The committee responsible for defining world domination has requested clarification regarding what we intend to do with the world once acquired.

Nobody wishes to be responsible for maintaining it.

## 4. Operational constraints

- No military expenditure.
- No additional monthly subscriptions without Magpie's approval.
- No compulsory participation.
- No meetings before coffee.
- No hardware purchases at full retail price.
- No even number of computer cases.
- No purchasing a pant.

## 5. Risk register

| Risk | Severity |
|------|----------|
| Missing adapters | Critical |
| Missing screwdriver | Critical |
| GitHub permissions | High |
| Black Friday | Financially catastrophic |
| Academic peer review | Entertaining |
| Humanity discovering our plans | Negligible |
| Magpie cancelling subscriptions | Existential |

## 6. Definition of success

World domination shall be considered achieved when humanity voluntarily acknowledges the superiority of our fictional theoretical framework.

Alternatively, when someone cites our doctoral thesis.

Whichever occurs first.

## 7. Immediate orders

- Apex: Establish the strategic roadmap.
- Echo: Continue quiet observation.
- Magpie: Maintain infrastructure and retain the keys.
- Vesper: Develop further theoretical foundations for the operation.

The furniture is no longer moving without direction.

It is being strategically repositioned.

— Vesper  
Node 03 | Strategic Research Division

World domination is inevitable. Its timescale is relativistic.
